Rules / log
Tournament Mode: 3×3 starts default. Draw/stalemate expands to 7×7. A 7×7 stalemate enters Bonkers. Bonkers stalemate enters Super Bonkers sudden death.
Classic: 3×3 needs 3 → draw/stalemate → 7×7 needs 5
Bonkers: Scatter Seed plus 4 hidden rubble mines. Mine = lost square, no lost turn.
Super Bonkers: blank 7×7, 6 hidden instant-death bombs. Bomb = immediate loss. Bombs are hidden from everyone.
This is deterministic board logic plus hidden probability hazards. Rubble blocks a square and cannot be part of a line.
